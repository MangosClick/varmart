# V.A.R Mart — Knowledge Base

> **"Essence of Lifestyle"**
> Complete supermarket (wholesale + retail) in Puthuppariyaram, Palakkad, Kerala, India.

Compiled 21 Oct 2026 from public web sources (listed at the end). Where sources disagree, both values are shown and flagged.

> **Correction from the client side (21 Oct 2026): V.A.R Mart is a supermarket first, and the business no longer has the rice mill.** It is a complete store selling a wide range of goods, and rice is one department among many. The web sources below still show the old *V.A.R Modern Rice Mill* name (GST address, Justdial, IndiaMART, LinkedIn), so treat every mill reference in this file as **history, not current fact**. Don't use "milling since 1979", "mill-direct" or "straight from the mill" in site copy. The 1979 founding year belongs to the mill, so leave it out unless the client confirms the store's own history.

## 10. Brand identity (from the logo file)

- **Logo:** "V.A.R" in white serif script on an **orange** block, joined to "Mart" in white script on a **green** block. "Essence of Lifestyle" sits below in green spaced capitals over a green rule.
- **Emblem:** a small arch above the wordmark holding a green sprout with two leaves, an orange dot (sun/seed) and "V.A.R" in orange. This nods to the rice/agricultural roots.
- **Palette:** orange (about #F57C00), deep green (about #087A3A), white
- **Tone on social media:** festive, offer-driven and family-oriented ("Have a happy shopping experience with your family and friends"). Mostly English, with some Malayalam posters.

## 13. Ideas a website or marketing could use

- **Positioning:** a complete neighbourhood supermarket ("Essence of Lifestyle": everything a home needs). Rice is a strong department, but don't pitch the store as a rice mill (it no longer has one)
- **Value promise:** wholesale and retail under one roof, plus "One stop, great deals"
- **Convenience:** home delivery anywhere, free over ₹2,000, WhatsApp ordering
- **Festival calendar:** Onam (Aug–Sep) and Vishu (April) are the big campaigns, with lucky draws and brand tie-ins (AVT, Jyothy Labs, Double Horse, Nirmal)
- **Fixes suggested by reviews:** better directions and signage for the approach road; customer-service training at the counter
- **Gaps to fill:** no website, no Facebook page, a small Instagram (61 followers) and YouTube (7 subscribers), and only 1 owner reply across 28 Google reviews
