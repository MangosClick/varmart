Who we are

V.A.R Mart is a wholesale and retail supermarket on Industrial Estate Road in Puthuppariyaram, Palakkad. Our tagline, Essence of Lifestyle, sums up what we try to be: everything everyday life needs, under one roof.

We stock what a household uses every week: Kerala rice and basmati, staples, cooking oils, tea and coffee, cleaning and personal care, plastic ware and stationery. Families shop with us retail, and shops and bulk buyers buy wholesale.

We keep prices fair, deliver to your door and take orders on WhatsApp. Onam and Vishu are the high points of our year, with our biggest offers and lucky draws.
